Privacy Policy

RC Car Twitch Extension

Effective date: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the RC Car Twitch Extension ("Extension") handles information when viewers use the Extension on Twitch.

2. Information processed

The Extension may process information provided by Twitch that is necessary to operate the Extension, including a Twitch-provided user identifier, authentication information, and commands sent through the Extension.

This information is used only to provide Extension functionality, such as determining which viewer currently has control of the RC car and forwarding control commands to the connected backend.

3. Data storage

The Extension is not designed to create user profiles or permanently store personal information. Operational data may be processed temporarily as needed to provide the service, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.

4. Payments

The Extension does not directly collect payment card or banking information. If payment or donation functionality is added, payment processing may be handled by a third-party provider under that provider's own privacy policy.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the Extension, comply with legal obligations, protect users or the service, or work with service providers that support the Extension's technical operation.

6. Twitch

Use of the Extension is also subject to Twitch's own terms and privacy practices. Twitch may collect and process information independently from this Extension.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the Extension and its backend. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Extension is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at this page.
